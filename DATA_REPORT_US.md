## Top 10 things to check against a real transcript
1. Exact grade table for the school/year (including A+)
2. Whether plus/minus used and points
3. Which grades excluded from GPA (P/NP, S/U, W, I, AU)
4. Repeat policy (replace/average/both/highest)
5. Term system (semester/quarter) and credit conversion
6. Transfer credits count toward institutional GPA?
7. Honors cutoffs if needed
8. School-level exceptions (e.g. Penn schools differ, Stanford exceptions)
9. Rounding policy
10. Whether GPA is published at all (Yale/Brown cases)
